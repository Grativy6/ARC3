# ARC3 Responsible-Use Position

ARC3 is retired. The author's strongest practical recommendation is **do not run or deploy it**.

The project is preserved publicly to retain an auditable research history, including limits and failures. Preservation is not endorsement. Public visibility, copyright permission applicable to an earlier snapshot, technical capability, or a benchmark result does not create ethical approval, consent, authority, or a safe-use determination.

The author does not approve use of ARC3 for autonomous real-world operations; access to credentials, private data, live services, production infrastructure, money, or physical systems; weapons, surveillance, coercion, manipulation, security exploitation, or hazardous work; high-impact decisions about people; self-propagation, hidden persistence, privilege acquisition, or unbounded delegation; or any setting without independently enforced limits, stopping, rollback, contest, and an accountable human decision-maker.

The fuller risk boundary and minimum precautions for independent historical reproduction are in [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md). This position does not purport to retract the historical MIT-0 grant recorded in [LICENSE](LICENSE), and it is not a substitute for technical containment, legal review, domain expertise, or the operator's own responsibility.

Any future written permission for first-party post-retirement material would be specific, scoped, and prospective. It would not by itself authorize deployment or consequence.
